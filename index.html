How It Works

1

Place Your iPhone

Set your iPhone on a nightstand or nearby surface before bed. The app guides you through optimal placement.

2

Start Monitoring

Tap Start. SnoreDetector periodically samples audio, analyzing it with on-device DSP for snore-like patterns.

3

Gentle Nudging

When snoring is detected, a soft sound cue or a watch tap encourages a natural position change — without disruption.

4

Wake Up Informed

Review your night summary. See snore frequency, intensity, and trends. Add notes to track what helps you sleep better.

Your Privacy Comes First

All audio processing happens on-device. No recordings, no analytics, no data ever leaves your iPhone. SnoreDetector was built so that your most private moments — your sleep — stay private.
